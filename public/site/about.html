About Next Step

Helping students take their next step with confidence.

As a registered nonprofit organization, we make the study-abroad journey clearer and more personal for students and families across Zimbabwe and Africa.

Our story

Guidance built around every student

Next Step Study Solutions is a registered nonprofit organization created to help students navigate university selection, applications, visa preparation and the move to a new country without feeling overwhelmed.

We combine practical guidance with personal, one-on-one support. From the first assessment to pre-departure preparation, our goal is to give every student clear information and a path they can trust.

Meet the founder

The person behind Next Step

Committed to opening doors to global education and helping students move forward with confidence.

Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

B.Mike Chimhuka

I started Next Step Study Solutions because I’ve been through the study-abroad journey myself. I’m currently an international student in the United States, and before moving here, I studied in Poland. From my own experience, I realized that a lot of Zimbabweans want the chance to study abroad, but many don’t know where to start or don’t have access to the right guidance and resources. I created Next Step to make the process easier, clearer and less stressful for students and their families.
