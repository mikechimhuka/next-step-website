Free for Zimbabwean students

Our guidance comes at no service fee.

As a registered nonprofit, Next Step Study Solutions provides Zimbabwean students with free guidance for school matching, applications, document preparation, scholarships, visa preparation and pre-departure planning.

Please note: Students are still responsible for costs charged by other organizations, including university application or tuition fees, visa fees, medical examinations, English tests, insurance, travel and accommodation.
