About Next Step

Helping students take their next step with confidence.

We make the study-abroad journey clearer and more personal for students and families across Zimbabwe and Africa.

Our story

Guidance built around every student

Next Step Study Solutions was created to help students navigate university selection, applications, visa preparation and the move to a new country without feeling overwhelmed.

We combine practical guidance with personal, one-on-one support. From the first assessment to pre-departure preparation, our goal is to give every student clear information and a path they can trust.
